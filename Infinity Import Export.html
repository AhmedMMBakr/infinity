How an order runs

From inquiry to unloading in five steps.

  1. Inquiry

    You tell us the product, quantity and destination port or address.

  2. Sample and spec

    We send the specification and, on request, a sample for approval.

  3. Quote

    Price as FOB or CIF, with payment terms and loading date.

  4. Inspection and loading

    Grading, packing and checks before loading at Alexandria or Damietta.

  5. Shipping and papers

    Sea freight to your port. Documents go ahead to you and your forwarder.

Documents with each shipment: commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate and, where applicable, EUR.1.
